## 🚀 About Me

I build the tooling my company runs on — and then keep building on my own time, because apparently I can't stop.

At **cfab** I own AI enablement across the business: internal full-stack apps for performance reporting, analytics, creator management and campaign planning, plus an agentic development workflow around them.

The path here: 4 years B2B sales (Indeed DACH), ~3 years B2B/B2C marketing and media buying, then a full pivot into AI-assisted development. No CS degree — just a generalist who kept shipping.

Off the clock: DJ and producer as **Hi-One**, co-founder of the **Soulside** collective in Düsseldorf.
